Fair Catan Boards

Generate balanced Catan maps for fun play.

Board Fairness Criteria
  • Desert Isolation: The desert tile is strictly prohibited from touching the outer water ring (ocean coastline).
  • Resource Dispersion: Maximum of 2 identical resource types can be adjacent to avoid massive mono-cultural territory clumps.
  • No Adjacent Identical Numbers: Identical numbers are completely barred from touching each other directly.
  • Red Token Non-Contiguity: High-yield numbers (6 & 8) cannot touch and are banned from being assigned to the same resource category.
  • Vertex Production Cap: No individual intersection spot can accumulate more than 11 total pips, blocking game-breaking initial settlements.
  • Strict Global Yield Equity: Total resource pips are dynamically normalized to minimize variance across categories to less than 10%.
  • Alternating Port Symmetrics: Maritime trade harbors rotate flawlessly around the coastline using a strict [3:1 ➔ Sea ➔ 2:1 ➔ Sea] cyclic rhythm.
